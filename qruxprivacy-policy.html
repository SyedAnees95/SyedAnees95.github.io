Privacy Policy

Qurux – QR Code Scanner & Generator ("Qurux", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Qurux mobile application.

Privacy at a glance: Qurux is designed to process QR code information locally on your device whenever possible and does not require you to create an account.

1. Information We Collect

Qurux does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the core QR scanning and generation features.

We do not intentionally collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Passwords
  • Contacts
  • Precise location
  • Personal identification information

QR codes that you scan or generate are processed locally on your device whenever possible.

2. Camera Permission

Qurux requires access to your device's camera to scan QR codes.

The camera is used only for QR code scanning. Qurux does not intentionally record, save, or upload camera footage to our servers.

You can revoke camera permission at any time through your Android device settings. If camera permission is denied, QR scanning functionality may not work.

3. QR Code Data

When you scan a QR code, the information contained in that QR code may be displayed or processed by the application.

QR codes may contain information such as:

  • Website URLs
  • Text
  • Wi-Fi information
  • Contact information
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Other QR-supported data

Qurux does not intentionally upload scanned QR code content to our own servers.

You are responsible for reviewing QR code content before opening links or sharing information.

4. QR Code Generation

Qurux allows you to generate QR codes from information that you enter into the application.

The information you provide for QR generation is used to create the QR code and is not intentionally transmitted to our servers.

5. Device Storage

If you choose to save generated QR codes or related content to your device, the information is stored locally on your device.

Qurux does not have access to files stored outside the permissions granted to the application.

6. Internet Access

Some functionality may require internet access, such as opening websites contained in QR codes or using external services initiated by you.

When you open an external website or service, that service's own privacy policy applies.

Qurux does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites or services.

7. Advertising and Third-Party Services

If advertisements or third-party services are included in a particular version of Qurux, those services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies.

For example, advertising providers may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, or advertising interaction data, subject to applicable laws and platform policies.

Users should review the privacy policies of any third-party services used by the application.

8. Children's Privacy

Qurux is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention

Because Qurux is designed to process QR-related information locally, we do not intentionally maintain a database of your scanned QR codes or generated QR code content on our servers.

Information saved locally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the application data, or uninstall the application, depending on your device and Android settings.

11. Your Choices

You can:

  • Deny or revoke camera permission.
  • Delete locally saved QR codes or generated images.
  • Clear the application's data through Android settings.
  • Uninstall Qurux from your device.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, services, or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
